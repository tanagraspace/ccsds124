CCSDS 124.0-B-1

🛰️ — — — — — 📡

Lossless compression for satellite housekeeping telemetry data.

The definitive implementation of the CCSDS 124.0-B-1 lossless compression algorithm of fixed-length housekeeping data.

Designed by the European Space Agency (ESA), implemented on the OPS-SAT-1 flight computer by georges.fyi, and flight validated in orbit, CCSDS 124.0-B-1 was then standardized by the Consultative Committee for Space Data Systems (CCSDS). The implementations here are also by georges.fyi at Tanagra Space, under the MIT License.
